# Vallshangout Brand Guidelines

## Brand direction

**Warm & Social** — a welcoming, modern, lifestyle-oriented identity for discovering places and experiences to hang out.

### Brand personality

- Friendly
- Relaxed
- Youthful
- Social
- Curious
- Approachable

### Brand promise

Help people discover their next good place and make hangout plans feel easy.

### Visual principles

1. **Warmth:** use warm cream backgrounds and orange accents to create an inviting atmosphere.
2. **Social energy:** use lively photography, concise copy, and clear calls to action.
3. **Room to breathe:** avoid crowded layouts; use generous spacing and simple hierarchy.
4. **Real places first:** photography and useful place information should be more prominent than decoration.
5. **Consistent interaction:** the same action and status should look the same throughout the product.

## Logo and brand color

- Primary brand color: Vallshangout Orange `#F97316`
- Supporting background: Warm Cream `#FFF7ED`
- Main text: Warm Charcoal `#292524`

Do not use the primary orange as the background for long blocks of text. Reserve it for key actions, highlights, and brand moments.

## Photography direction

Prefer:
- Cafe interiors, food, casual activities, and welcoming hangout spaces
- Natural or warm lighting
- Authentic scenes and approachable compositions
- Images that communicate atmosphere and the experience

Avoid:
- Overly staged corporate stock imagery
- Heavy filters that distort the place's actual appearance
- Images with text overlays that compete with UI copy

## UI shape and layout

- Cards: white surfaces, subtle border, rounded corners
- Buttons: rounded, clearly labeled, orange for primary action
- Search: prominent and easy to discover
- Categories: compact chips with clear labels
- Layout: clean, content-led, mobile-friendly

## Voice and microcopy

Use short, friendly, natural language. Avoid overly formal or enterprise-style wording.

Examples:
- “Cari tempat nongkrong”
- “Temukan spot baru”
- “Yuk, explore!”
- “Lihat tempat”

## Accessibility

- Do not communicate status through color alone; include labels or icons.
- Check text contrast for every color combination.
- Keep focus states visible.
- Prefer clear labels over ambiguous icon-only controls.
